Our Story

Founded in 2010, AutoCare Pro has been serving the community with dedication and expertise for over 15 years. What started as a small family-owned garage has grown into a trusted name in auto repair, known for quality service and customer satisfaction.

We believe in building long-term relationships with our customers through honest communication, transparent pricing, and exceptional workmanship. Every vehicle that comes through our doors receives the same level of care and attention we'd give our own.

Our commitment to staying current with automotive technology means we invest in the latest diagnostic equipment and ongoing training for our technicians. This ensures we can handle everything from classic cars to the newest models with equal expertise.
